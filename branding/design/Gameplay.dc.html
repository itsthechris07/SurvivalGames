More than loot and fight

Every part has its settings in config.yml – the extras can be turned off or used in the other game type.

Map voting

The players vote for the map in the lobby. A permission gives up to 5 votes.

Kits

Admins save their inventory as a kit, the players pick one in the lobby with /sg kit.

Loot chests

Filled when opened, refilled every few minutes, better loot near the center. All in loot.yml.

Grace & deathmatch

A peaceful start, lightnings that reveal who hides, then back to the spawns in a closing border.

Tracker & supply drops

A compass points at the nearest enemy. Chests with supply loot land near the center.

Sponsors & bounties

Spectators send items to the players alive. Put money on a player – the killer gets it.

Teams

Team menu or /sg team create, team colors, team chat with @message, parties stay together.

Spectators

Players who are out and visitors watch invisibly. A click shows the inventory of your killer.

Varo play time

A few minutes per day on the scoreboard – at set play hours if you like. A smaller border every day.

One life

Out after the first death. No logout during a fight: a villager stands in for you.

Varo rules

Strikes, peaceful days, daily coordinates, banned items, potions and enchantments.

Extras

Backpack, chest lock with a [Lock] sign, gravity grenade and toxic rain – per game type.
