Ultra Hardcore

A new world every round, no loot and no natural regeneration – mine, craft and be the last team standing.

UHC

one round · new world every time
1
Lobby & generation
While the players wait, the world inside the border is generated – the more teams, the bigger it gets.
2
Scatter
Every team starts at a random place on dry land, far away from the others – never in the ocean or on a tree.
3
Grace period
Ten minutes without PvP to mine and craft. The time left is shown above the hotbar.
4
Final heal & border
Everybody alive gets full health once, then the world border pushes all teams into the center.
5
Last team standing wins
The dead watch the rest of the round. Then the replay is ready and the next round gets a new world.
