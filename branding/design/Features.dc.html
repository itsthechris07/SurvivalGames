Ready for your server

Several arenas on one server – or one game per server behind Velocity or BungeeCord.

Any map

A folder in maps/, an imported or a generated world. Every round gets a fresh copy.

Set up in game

Spawn points or a spawn circle, lobby, center and world border – in an edit copy of the map.

Menus

/sg is the main menu of the players, /sg admin sets up arenas, games and settings.

Three server modes

Games on the side, one game per server, or a lobby that sends players to free servers.

Join signs & NPCs

[sg] signs and NPCs of Citizens or FancyNpcs show the state and join with a click.

Statistics

Wins, kills and an Elo rating, in a file or MySQL. /sg top, holograms and [sgtop] signs.

Replays

Every round and every Varo day is recorded and played again in a copy of the map. No client mod.

Rewards

Money and console commands for kills, wins and games, fireworks for the winners.

Achievements

First win, 3 kills in a game, a win without armor, a Varo without a strike and more.

Varo backups

The world is zipped at the start of every day. A command goes back to a backup.

Integrations

Vault, Parties, TAB, EssentialsX, Multiverse – and forms for Bedrock players with Floodgate.

Your texts

English and German, MiniMessage with clickable commands, PlaceholderAPI %sg_…%.
