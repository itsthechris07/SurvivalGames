Ready for your server

Several arenas on one server – or one game per server behind Velocity or BungeeCord.

Any map

A folder in maps/, an imported or a generated world. Every round gets a fresh copy.

Set up in game

Spawn points or a spawn circle, lobby, center and world border – in an edit copy of the map.

Loot chests

Filled when opened, refilled every few minutes, better loot near the center. All in loot.yml.

Grace & deathmatch

A peaceful start, then back to the spawns while the world border closes in.

Varo play time

A few minutes per day on the scoreboard. Every new day brings a smaller border.

One life

Out after the first death. No logout during a fight, join protection, revive by an admin.

Teams

Team menu or /sg team create, team colors, team chat with @message.

Spectators

Players who are out and visitors watch invisibly – /sg spectate.

Three server modes

Games on the side, one game per server, or a lobby that sends players to free servers.

Join signs

[sg] signs show the state and let players join with a click – on one server or across the network.

Extras

Backpack, chest lock with a [Lock] sign, gravity grenade and toxic rain – per game type.

Your texts

English and German, MiniMessage with clickable commands, PlaceholderAPI %sg_…%.
