Two games, one plugin

Every arena has a game type: quick rounds with a fresh map – or a Varo that runs for days in the same world.

SURVIVAL GAMES

one round · fresh map every time
1
Lobby
Pick a team. The countdown starts once enough players are there – shorter when the game is full.
2
Spawn points
Everybody stands on a spawn point around the center, frozen until the start countdown is over.
3
Grace period & loot
A minute of peace to loot the chests – refilled every few minutes, better loot near the center.
4
Deathmatch
After 20 minutes or with few players left: back to the spawns, the world border shrinks.
5
Last one standing wins
The dead watch as spectators. Then the next round starts on a fresh copy of the map.

Minutes per day
Join, play your time, leave. Outside of the game you get your own inventory back.
One life
The first death is the last one. When the last player of a team is out, the team is out.
No escape
With an enemy nearby the time doesn’t run out. Join protection after coming back.
Shrinking world
Every new day the border gets smaller. The world and the game survive every restart.
