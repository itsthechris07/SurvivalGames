Rounds or days

Every arena has a game type: quick rounds with a fresh map – or a Varo that runs for days in the same world.

SURVIVAL GAMES

one round · fresh map every time
1
Lobby
Vote for the map, pick a kit and a team. The countdown starts once enough players are there.
2
Spawn points & grace period
Everybody stands frozen on a spawn point around the center. Then a minute of peace to loot the chests.
3
The hunt
A compass tracks the nearest enemy, supply drops land near the center and lightnings reveal who hides.
4
Deathmatch
After 20 minutes or with few players left: back to the spawns, the world border shrinks.
5
Last one standing wins
The dead watch and sponsor items. Afterwards the replay is ready and the next round starts on a fresh map.
